01 — Summer Phases

Three phases of progression

Phase 1
Foundation
1 June – 28 June
Establish the daily routine and posture habits. All fundamentals at comfortable speeds. Daily routine is non-negotiable before any technical work begins.
Remington: 70 → 80 bpm
Chromatic: 60 → 70 bpm
Lip slurs: 30 → 40 bpm
Phase 2
Building
29 June – 26 July
Increase tempos steadily. Scale work takes priority. Develop register connection. Excerpt work ramps up. Maintain ease from Phase 1 while adding complexity.
Remington: 80 → 90 bpm
Chromatic: 70 → 80 bpm
Lip slurs: 40 → 50 bpm
Phase 3
Integration
27 July – 31 August
Hit all tempo targets. Polish excerpts. Recital repertoire consolidation. Complete professional prep. The whole summer's work comes together.
Remington: 90 → 100 bpm
Chromatic: 80 → 90 bpm
Lip slurs: 50 → 60 bpm
